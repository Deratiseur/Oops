Introduction

Oops is a magician who lives in the south-western part of the exterior area of Watcher's Keep. You can meet him in both Shadows of Amn and Throne of Bhaal.

He turns arcane spell scrolls into special abilities usable once per day.

Version 5 adds Blips, his imp familiar, as an optional component. Blips offers his services exclusively to sorcerers, allowing them to know additional spells.

The scrolls must be in the party's inventories. Having a spell memorised or recorded in a spellbook does not replace the required scroll.

Oops: special abilities

  • Start the conversation with the character who should receive the ability.
  • Bring the scroll for the desired spell and enough gold and experience.
  • The scroll is consumed. The character gains the ability to cast the spell once per day, with a casting time of 1.
  • You can repeat the service as long as you have the required resources.
CostFor a level N spell
Scrolls1
Gold5,000 × N
XP1,000 × N

Example: requesting Stoneskin as a special ability costs one scroll of Stoneskin, 20,000 gold pieces and 4,000 XP.

To access Oops's service menu, the character must have at least 10,000 XP and the party at least 5,000 gold pieces. The actual cost of each ability is as shown above.

Blips: more known spells

Blips is an imp found near Oops. Only sorcerers can use his services. The main Oops component must be installed before you can install Blips.

In exchange for three scrolls of the same spell, gold and experience, he adds that spell directly to the sorcerer's known spells.

CostFor a level N spell
Scrolls3
Gold2,000 × N
XP2,000 × N
  • Spells already known are not offered.
  • Sometimes a spell has several different scrolls. Blips's recipe can use any of them, and even mix them.
  • Blips can teach a sorcerer a spell of a higher level than they currently have access to.

Example: asking to know a level 9 spell costs three scrolls, 18,000 gold pieces and 18,000 XP. A sorcerer who already knows three level 9 spells can therefore know a fourth, then a fifth… as long as they can pay.

Compatibility and installation order

In theory, there should be no incompatibilities: this mod is “green”; it does not modify any base-game files in a way that could interfere with other mods.

It supports all the following spells:

  • Base-game arcane spells.
  • Icewind Dale spells added by IWDification or SCS.
  • New arcane spells from SCS 35 and later.
  • Interplan (version 10 for the updated spell list).
  • Olvyn’s Spells.
  • Made in Heaven: Spellpack (MIH Spells).
  • Dragon Summoning.
  • Spacewarp, from Test Your Mettle.

Spells from other mods are recognised only if those mods are installed before Oops.

Installation and uninstallation

  1. Install the spell mods you want Oops to recognise first.
  2. Extract the Derats_Oops folder and Setup-Derats_Oops.exe into the game folder, next to chitin.key.
  3. Run Setup-Derats_Oops.exe, choose your language and install Oops.
  4. Then install Blips if you want this optional component.

To change the installation or uninstall the mod, run the same program again. After changes to creatures or their spawning, use a save from before your first visit to the Watcher's Keep area for testing.
